From 9.00 AM to 8.00 PM IST,7 days of week Support

Sphurti Electrical Shop provides customer support from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST, 7 days a week. Our support team is available to help customers with product information, orders, payments, delivery, warranty, returns, and other general queries.
Customers can contact us through phone, email, or other available communication channels. We aim to understand customer problems and provide clear and helpful solutions as quickly as possible. For faster assistance, customers should provide their order details or relevant product information when contacting our support team.

Payment Guide

Sphurti Electrical Shop provides multiple payment options to make purchasing convenient for customers. Depending on product and order eligibility, customers can make payments using credit cards, debit cards, UPI, net banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
Customers should carefully check the order details, product quantity, total price, and selected payment method before completing the payment. If a payment fails or the amount is deducted but the order is not confirmed, customers can contact our support team with the relevant transaction or order details for assistance.

Installment Purchase Guide

Sphurti Electrical Shop may provide installment or EMI payment options for eligible products and customers. Installment payments allow customers to purchase selected products and pay the amount over a specified period instead of making the complete payment at once.
Customers can check the available EMI options during the checkout process. The installment period, applicable charges, eligibility, and terms may vary depending on the payment provider, bank, product, and order value. Customers should review all applicable terms before confirming an installment purchase.

Print E-Invoices

After successfully completing a purchase, customers can receive an electronic invoice containing important order and purchase information. The invoice may include details such as product name, quantity, price, taxes, order information, and payment details.
Customers can download or print the e-invoice for their records and future reference. It is recommended to keep the invoice safely because it may be required for warranty claims, product returns, replacements, or other support-related requests.

Warranty Lookup

Customers can check the warranty status of eligible products using their order ID, invoice details, or other required purchase information. The warranty period and coverage may differ depending on the product and manufacturer.
The warranty lookup helps customers understand whether their product is currently covered under warranty. If customers have difficulty finding their warranty information or have questions regarding warranty coverage, they can contact our support team and provide the purchase or product details for further assistance.
