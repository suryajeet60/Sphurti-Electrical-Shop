Sphurti Electrical Shop - General Policy

Shipping Policy | Warranty Policy | Policy For Business | Checking Policy | Customer Information Security

Shipping Policy

Sphurti Electrical Shop aims to provide safe, reliable, and timely delivery of all purchased products. Orders are generally processed and shipped within 2–3 business days after confirmation. Delivery time may vary depending on the customer’s location, product availability, and delivery service.
All products are carefully packed before shipping to reduce the possibility of damage during transportation. Customers should provide accurate contact and delivery information while placing an order. In case of any delay or delivery-related issue, customers can contact our support team for assistance and order updates.

Warranty Policy

Sphurti Electrical Shop provides warranty support for eligible electrical products purchased from our shop. Most applicable products come with a minimum 1-year warranty, while the actual warranty period and conditions may vary depending on the product and manufacturer.
Warranty coverage generally applies to manufacturing defects and does not normally cover damage caused by improper installation, misuse, accidents, unauthorized repairs, voltage fluctuations, or normal wear and tear. Customers should keep their bill or proof of purchase safely for warranty claims.

Policy For Business

Sphurti Electrical Shop supports businesses, contractors, offices, shops, and other organizations that require electrical products in larger quantities. Business customers can contact us for bulk orders, product availability, pricing, and delivery requirements.
We aim to provide competitive pricing and suitable solutions according to the requirements of business customers. For large orders, delivery schedules and payment terms may be discussed and confirmed before the order is processed. We also try to provide priority assistance and proper communication for business-related requirements.

Checking Policy

All products are checked before being packed and shipped to ensure that they are in proper condition. We make reasonable efforts to verify the product, quantity, packaging, and visible condition before dispatching an order.
Customers are advised to check the package and product carefully after delivery. If a product is received damaged, defective, incorrect, or incomplete, the customer should contact our support team as soon as possible, preferably within 48 hours of receiving the order. Photographs or other relevant details may be requested to help resolve the issue quickly.

Customer Information Security

Sphurti Electrical Shop respects the privacy and security of its customers. Personal information such as customer name, phone number, email address, and delivery details is used for purposes such as processing orders, providing customer support, and communicating important information related to purchases.
We do not intentionally sell or share customer personal information with unauthorized third parties. Appropriate security measures are used to help protect customer information and payment-related data. Customers are also advised not to share passwords, OTPs, PINs, or other confidential payment information with anyone claiming to represent the shop.
